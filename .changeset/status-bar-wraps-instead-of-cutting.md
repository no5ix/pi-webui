The line at the bottom of the window wraps instead of cutting. It carries tokens,
context, the git branch, how long the last task ran, what the extensions report,
and cost - and a bar that clips one of them leaves the reader with a number whose
meaning got cut. Nothing is shortened with an ellipsis any more, the order stays
the same at every width, and the bar grows to as many lines as its content needs.

PI WEB's two built-in looks are named **Darkness** and **Lightness**. The ids stay `core:builtin-dark` and `core:builtin-light`, so a shared-theme config, a plugin, a deep link and a saved preference all keep working; the names in **Settings → Appearance** are now the ones a reader would use out loud.
